More Than a Coffee Shop

Java Been is designed to be a welcoming space for more than coffee. Guests can enjoy local music, creative events, quiet study time, and casual meetups throughout the week.

Whether you are stopping in for a drink or looking for a place to connect with the community, Java Been offers a relaxed environment for work, creativity, and conversation.
