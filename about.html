About Java Been

A cozy coffee house built around fresh drinks, warm food, and a welcoming atmosphere.

Our Story

Java Been is a local coffee house designed for people who want a comfortable place to relax, study, meet with friends, or grab a fresh drink on the go. The website design focuses on a warm, simple, and inviting experience that matches the feeling of the coffee shop.

The goal of this site is to make important information easy to find, including menu items, featured favorites, online ordering, and contact information. The layout is responsive so visitors can use the website from a phone, tablet, or desktop computer.
